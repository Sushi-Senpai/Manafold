### 7. Email hygiene

    {"error":"an account with that email already exists"}
    
    << HTTP 409 >>

    {"error":"a valid email is required"}
    
    << HTTP 422 >>
  7c. password under 10 chars -> 422:
    {"error":"password must be at least 10 characters"}
    
    << HTTP 422 >>
